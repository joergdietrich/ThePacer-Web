The Pacer is a running metronome for interval sessions. You define a session once — warm-up, reps, recoveries and cool-down — and the app takes you through it. During each rep it plays a beat at the cadence that puts you on your target pace. Put a foot down on every beat, and the pace takes care of itself.

Everything else in the app supports the beat. A voice counts you down to the end of each rep, a gauge shows your pace if you want to look, and a summary afterwards tells you how close you stayed.
