title: The pace gauge
---

The beat does the pacing. The gauge is there for when you want to check. It sits on the pace card during every rep and answers one question at a glance: are you on pace, and if not, which way are you off?

<figure>
  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/screens/08-training-rep.png' | relative_url }}" width="390" height="844" alt="The pace gauge reading slightly slow.">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/screens/13-training-rep-dark.png' | relative_url }}" width="390" height="844" alt="The pace gauge in dark mode.">
</figure>

- **Needle straight right:** you are on pace.
- **Needle up, towards FAST:** you are faster than the target. Ease off.
- **Needle down, towards SLOW:** you are slower than the target. Pick it up.

The colour behind the needle runs from dark green on pace, through light green and yellow, to red when you are well off. A chip below the gauge says the same in words, for example **Slightly slow · +0:11 /km**, so you never have to rely on colour.

GPS pace wanders a little even when you hold a steady speed, so expect the needle to move now and then. If it settles off target, check that you are still stepping with the beat.
