A session is one workout, defined once and run as often as you like. It has four phases:

| Phase | What it is | Choices |
|---|---|---|
| Warm-up | Easy running before the reps | Time, distance, open, or none |
| Training | The reps you run at a target pace, to the beat | Time or distance, a pace, and 1 to 99 repetitions |
| Interval | The recovery between reps | Time or distance, with or without a target pace |
| Cool-down | Easy running at the end | Time, distance, open, or none |

The target pace is what sets the beat: the app turns it into the cadence you step to during the rep.

An **open** warm-up or cool-down has no length. It runs until you tap Next, or Finish at the end.

## Defining a session

On the Start screen, tap **Define Interval Session**, or **New** in the bottom bar.

<figure>
  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/screens/05-define-session.png' | relative_url }}" width="390" height="1400" alt="The form for defining a session.">
  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/screens/01-start.png' | relative_url }}" width="390" height="844" alt="The Start screen.">
</figure>

- **Name.** With **Auto-fill name** on, the app names the session from its contents, for example `6 × 400m @ 4:30/km / 1:30min`: six reps of 400 m at 4:30 per kilometre, with 1:30 recoveries. Turn it off to type your own name.
- **Distances and paces** carry a unit inside the field. Tap the unit to change it: m, yd, km and mi for a distance, /km and /mi for a pace. Changing the unit keeps the number you typed.
- **Times and paces** fill from the right as you type digits, because a phone's number pad has no colon. Typing 1, 3, 0 gives `1:30`.
- **Interval after last training** adds a recovery after the final rep. It is off by default, so six reps have five recoveries.
- **No target pace** is checked by default for the interval, so recoveries are jogged or walked at any pace. Uncheck it to give recoveries a pace of their own, with a beat and the gauge.

Tap **Save**. The app stores the session and takes you straight to Start training. If a field is missing or invalid, Save marks it and scrolls to it.

A value keeps the unit you entered it in. A 400 yd rep stays 400 yd, even with the display set to metric, because a 400 m rep and a 440 yd rep are different workouts.

## Saved sessions

Tap **Load Interval Session**, or **Sessions** in the bottom bar, to see your saved sessions. Tap a card to go to Start training with that session.

<figure>
  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/screens/02-load-session.png' | relative_url }}" width="390" height="844" alt="The list of saved sessions.">
</figure>

- **Sort** by date or by name, either way round. The app remembers your choice.
- **Edit** opens the form with the session filled in. Editing a session does not change runs you have already done with it.
- **Clone** copies the session at once and adds a number to its name, for example `(1)`.
- **Delete** asks first. After deleting, **Undo** in the bar at the bottom brings the session back.

Sessions are stored on your phone only.
