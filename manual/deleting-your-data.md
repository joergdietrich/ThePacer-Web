---

The Pacer keeps your sessions and runs on your phone. If you turned on *Share my runs*, some data about them is also on our server. This chapter says how to delete each. The [privacy policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}) lists exactly what is stored and sent.

## On your phone

- **A run.** In the history, tap the bin on its row and confirm. Its recording and .fit file go with it.
- **A session.** In the session list, choose **Delete** and confirm.
- **Your stride.** In Settings, **Reset my stride** clears what the app learned about it.
- **Everything.** Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone.

A .fit file you sent with **Share .fit file** is a copy that now lives with the app or service you sent it to. Delete it there.

## On our server

If you turned on *Share my runs*, everything sent is grouped under your **install ID**, a random ID the app made when you agreed. It is not linked to your name, your Google account or your phone. Settings shows it under **Share my runs**, with a button to copy it.

Everything sent is deleted from the server automatically 12 months after it arrived. To delete it sooner:

  <img src="{{ '/assets/img/screens/63-settings-sharing-pending.png' | relative_url }}" width="390" height="844" alt="Settings with a deletion requested and waiting for the phone to be online.">

1. In Settings, turn off **Share my runs**.
2. The app asks what to do with what was already sent:
   - **Stop and delete what was sent** stops sharing and deletes it.
   - **Stop sharing** stops sharing and keeps it. The install ID stays in Settings, with **Delete what was sent** beside it, so you can delete later.
3. If the phone is offline, the deletion waits and goes the next time it is online. Until the server confirms it, the install ID stays in Settings with a line saying the deletion is requested. When the ID disappears, the deletion is done.

Deleting cannot be undone. If you share again later, the app starts a new install ID.

## After uninstalling

Uninstalling the app does not delete what was already sent. To have it deleted, e-mail your install ID to [info@thepacer.app](mailto:info@thepacer.app) and we delete everything under it.

**Write the install ID down before you uninstall.** Copy it from Settings and keep it somewhere safe. Our server knows your data only by this ID, never by who you are, and uninstalling deletes the app's copy of it. Once the app is gone and the ID is lost, nobody, including us, can tell which data is yours, so we cannot find it to delete it early. It is then deleted with everything else, 12 months after it was sent.
