While a session runs, two buttons sit at the top right of every training screen: **Pause** and **Abort**.

## Pause

Tap Pause and time and distance stop counting. The phase stays where it is, and the spoken cues go quiet. A **Paused** chip appears, with **Resume** and **Abort** at the bottom of the screen. Resume carries on where you left off. Paused time never counts towards a phase or an average pace.

## Auto-pause

When you stop moving — at a traffic light, or to tie a shoe — the session pauses by itself. The line under the title reads **Auto-paused · resumes when you move**, and it resumes when you set off again.

After you pause by hand, the session stays paused until you tap Resume, even if you walk a few steps.

## Abort

Abort throws the run away. It asks first, and after you confirm it takes you back to Start training with the same session, ready to run again. Nothing is saved: an aborted run leaves no history and no file.

The back gesture during a session works like Abort and asks the same question, so a stray swipe cannot lose your run. While the dialog is open, the session keeps running.

## Finish

The normal way to end is **Finish** in the cool-down. It works from a paused cool-down as well. A session also ends by itself when its last timed or distance phase runs out.

If you forget a session and leave it paused for half an hour, the app finishes it for you and keeps the run.
